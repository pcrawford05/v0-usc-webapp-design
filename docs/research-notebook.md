## Where facts actually live (go here first)

- **LinkedIn host pages beat websites for current dates.** Greif (`/company/lloydgreifcenter/`), TIE Hub (`/company/usc-viterbi-tie/`), IYA (`/school/usciovineyoung/`), Stevens (`/company/usc-stevens-center-for-innovation/`), Keck MESH (`/company/usc-mesh-strategic-partnerships/`), Brittingham (`/company/usc-brittingham-social-enterprise-lab/`), USCVC (`/company/usc-venture-capital/`). Brittingham's page also posts Greif Incubator events (Start Up Salon).
- **LinkedIn company search** finds USC clubs fast (search "USC" plus a topic). Post search for program names returns mostly personal posts, which we do not use; skip it.
- **EngageSC directory search** (`club_signup?view=all&search=`) is public and gives an RSO's mission and membership terms; it is the quickest activity check for clubs.
- **Marshall minors page** (students.marshall.usc.edu/current-students/applying-marshall-current-usc-students/marshall-minors) lists every Marshall minor for the current catalogue.
- **provost.sma.usc.edu/prog/** and **calendar.usc.edu** carry OORI programs and their deadlines.
- **Blocked or JavaScript-only:** viterbiinnovation.usc.edu and some CS newsletter pages block curl (Cloudflare; use WebFetch or a browser). iovine-young.usc.edu, Engage event pages and uscventurecapital.com need a browser. catalogue.usc.edu sometimes fails with a certificate error.
